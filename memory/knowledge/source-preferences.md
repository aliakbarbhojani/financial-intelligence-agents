# Source preferences (by data type)

Defaults for all jurisdictions. Add jurisdiction-specific sources as you use the system.

| Data type | Preferred (Tier 1) | Acceptable (Tier 2) |
|---|---|---|
| Company financials | Regulatory filings: SEC EDGAR (US), SEDAR+ (CA), Companies House / FCA NSM (UK), ESEF filings (EU), ASX announcements (AU); company IR | Reputable data providers, quoting the filing period |
| Policy rates and central-bank guidance | The relevant central bank's own statements and minutes | Major financial press summarising them |
| Inflation, employment, GDP | National statistics agency; FRED for US series | IMF/OECD/World Bank for cross-country comparison |
| Market prices | Exchange data; reputable market-data providers (state delayed vs live) | Financial press, with timestamp |
| Consumer lending rules | Financial consumer-protection regulator; central bank | Recognised consumer-finance education sites |
| Tax treatment | National tax authority guidance | Professional bodies' summaries, marked general info |
| Scam / registration checks | Securities regulator registries and investor-warning lists | Established consumer-protection organisations |

Avoid as evidence: content farms, affiliate "best of" lists, AI-generated summaries without sources, influencer income claims, and press releases presented as news.
